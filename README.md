## Overview

The uSurvive plugin adds most of base mechanics and functionality for my server.

This repository was originally built for personal use and was not intended to be public, so some commit history and internal structure may be rough around the edges.

## Features
- Animal Behaviour
  - Animals have a chance to attack back when a player hits them
  - When an animal is attacked, any nearby animals may also flee or have a chance to attack
  - The flee movement speed of all animals has been increased
- Sleeping
  - Time moves faster when a player is sleeping in a bed instead of instantly skipping the night
  - Players may sleep in a bed during the day
  - Food gets drained quickly while sleeping but the player's health increases
- Potions
  - 34 new custom potions ranging from simple ones such as Architect (increases building reach), to Tendril Grasp (Increases attack reach and slightly heals when attacking entities)
- TPA Mechanics
  - Adds the ability to request to teleport to a player or request a player to teleport to you
- Enchants
  - 35 new custom enchants
- Gravestones
  - When a player dies, a gravestone mob will get spawned, called the Echo
  - The Echo will hold on to the player's items and experience
  - The player may right click on the Echo to open a GUI to get back their items
  - To get back all of their experience, the player must kill the Echo
- Crux Item Components
  - A whole bunch of item components such as the bone flute component (which plays a sequence of sounds before shooting out a shockwave that damages mobs and players) and blast tempest (locks onto a target for x amount of time before spawning TNT on the target's last location)
- And some more...
  - From new advancements, custom blocks, and attributes- this is essentially the base of uSurvive
